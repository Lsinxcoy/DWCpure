## 二、★★ 数据获取是入口，其性质**限定后续所有阶段的上界**

**§2.5.4 原文逐字**：

```text
"**data acquisition is the entry point** of the self-improvement loop, because
 self-improvement presupposes experiences to learn from: **no later stage—selection,
 optimization, refinement, or evaluation—has anything to operate on** until data has
 been acquired."

"Because it stands at the **head of the data flow**, the properties of the acquired data,
 such as its **scale, diversity, difficulty, and noise**, propagate through the entire
 loop and **bound what any later stage can achieve**."

"This positions autonomous data acquisition **not as a one-time preprocessing step**,
 but as a **persistent, model-driven process** that restarts the loop at each iteration"
```

★ **"bound what any later stage can achieve"** 是硬论断：
  任务源决定了进化的天花板 ⇒ 对应 DWC §9 第 4 项（任务源）。
  ★ 且与 2607.07663 的验证层级形成上下夹击：
    上界由**任务源的接地质量**决定，下界由**信号可靠性层级**决定。

## 三、★★★★★ Coupled / Decoupled —— 第 20 项的教科书形式化

### 3.1 定义（§3.3.3 原文逐字）

```text
"**In a coupled setting**, the main model is updated on the selected data, and its
 **changed state feeds back into the next round of signal generation**, creating an
 interleaved loop in which **selection and optimization co-adapt**."

"Coupling arises naturally when the selector's signal is derived from **the main model
 being optimized**—for instance, policy advantages in RL-based curricula, data
 influence probed from **evolving pretraining checkpoints**, or validation performance
 tracked across fine-tuning steps."

"**In a decoupled setting**, the selector is trained **independently**—often via
 bilevel optimization on a held-out validation objective, through a separate
 policy-gradient or scoring phase, or **using a proxy model to provide reward signals**—
 and the resulting selection is applied **before** the main model begins training."

"**Decoupled designs reduce system complexity, but forfeit the ability to adapt selection
 as the model's needs shift during training.**"
```

### 3.2 Table 4：14 个方法实测分类（Coupled 9 / Decoupled 5）

```text
Coupled（9）:  MATES(Influence) · RAISE(Loss,RL) · ScalingRL(RL) · APO(Uncertainty,Bandit)
               SEAL ✗Decoupled · Grangier(Group,Loss,Bilevel) · DUMP(Bandit)
               SEC(Bandit) · SPaCe(Bandit) · Group-MATES(Influence) · DynamixSFT(Bandit)
Decoupled(5): SEAL(Bilevel) · Multi-Actor(RL) · DATAMASK(RL) · RL-Guided(RL)
               ScaleBiO(Bilevel)
★ 表头维度: Unit(Instance/Group) × Signal × Update(Supervised/RL/Bandit/Bilevel/Learned)
             × **Coupling**
★ ★ 原文: "**The majority of surveyed methods adopt this design**"（Coupled）
```

### 3.3 ★★★★ 决定性论断 + 被点名的空白

```text
"Notably, all surveyed methods in this regime rely on a **separate selector
 module**—whether a small influence model, a trainable MLP scorer, a bandit policy,
 or a bilevel-optimized ranker—**while a distinct main model is optimized** on the
 selected data.

 ★★★★★ "No existing method in this regime has the main model curate its own training
   data without an external selector."

 This gap points to an underexplored direction for future work: **self-directed
 selection**, in which a single model simultaneously acts as both **selector and
 learner**, representing the **strongest form of autonomy** in self-improving systems."
```

★★ **这是本次深读对 DWC 最重要的一条**：

```text
① DWC §9 第 20 项（守卫① 用世界模型自算 confidence）在本文献里的名字叫
   "coupled selector"，且是**主流设计**（9/14）—— 不是少数派错误
② 但同一篇明确说: **没有任何已发表方法让主模型无外部选择器地自我选择**，
   并把这种形态（self-directed selection）点名为"最强自主形态"且是空白
★★★ ⇒ 两句话合起来的读法:
   · Coupling 在**数据选择**域是主流且已知有偏置风险
   · DWC 想做的（同一状态既执行又评估）正是被点名的空白
   ⇒ ★★ DWC 若真做到，必须说明凭什么不重蹈 coupled 的偏置
     —— 而答案就在 2608.03392 §10.2: 自评分数近满分而真实性能不升反降（六模型）
```

### 3.4 另一维度：Curriculum-Driven vs Objective-Driven（§3.3.4）

```text
"**Curriculum-driven selection** aims to select data that is most appropriate for the
 model's **current learning stage**. ... 'appropriate' does not simply mean high-quality
 or high-reward; rather, it refers to examples whose **difficulty or informational value
 matches the model's current capacity—neither too trivial to provide new learning
 signal, nor too difficult to be learnable**"

  ★ DWC 的 WORLD_TREE 收束"选信息增益"就是在做这件事，只是没写成这个形式
```

```text
(v)    Evaluation Bottlenecks（评估瓶颈）

(vi)   Supervision Bottlenecks（监督瓶颈）
```

### 3·2 §7.1 的确认偏误（与第 20 项直接对应）

```text
"**In wild environments, autonomous agents exhibit a confirmation bias**, ignoring new
 information to favor their pre-existing training defaults (Trehan & Chopra, 2026)."

"In synthetic settings, the data selection process is constrained by the model's
 current capabilities. This **self-selection tends to reduce linguistic diversity
 while retaining noise** (Guo et al., 2024b), and **can amplify hallucinations as
 models preferentially select outputs that align with their internal parametric errors
 rather than factual ground truth** (Huang et al., 2025c)."

"Theoretically, Xiao et al. (2025) show that **optimal data selection requires a reliable
 validation signal, which is often unavailable in autonomous settings**, rendering the
 optimization problem **intractable**."
```

★★★★ **这是第 20 项最重的一条理论论断**：

```text
① 确认偏误 = "偏好内部参数错误而非事实基准" = 自评的根本缺陷
② ★★ 理论层面: "optimal data selection **requires a reliable validation signal**"
   —— 而自主设定里**这个信号通常不可得** ⇒ **优化问题不可解**（intractable）
⇒ ★★ DWC 的守卫① 若保留 coupled 设计，必须正面回答:
  DWC 的"可靠验证信号"从哪来？若答不出，理论上该设计不成立。
⇒ 这不是"文献不支持"，是"理论上不可解" —— 必须写进 ARCHITECTURE 的风险栏
```

### 3·4 为什么需要自主评估（两压力 + 一句定义）

```text
"without a robust evaluation framework, it becomes unclear whether observed
 improvements reflect **genuine capability growth, transient overfitting, or
 exploitation of feedback signals**."

"When a model continuously updates its parameters, modifies prompts, or adapts its
 inference policies, a **fixed benchmark can quickly become obsolete**. Performance gains
 may reflect overfitting to known test distributions rather than broader competence."

"**autonomous evaluation**" 的定义 —— 在两压力下仍保持信息量的评估协议:
  (i)  **temporal drift** in data and task distributions（新知识、用户需求变化）
  (ii) **adaptive pressure** from increasingly agentic systems that can
       "exploit, overfit to, or otherwise render fixed test sets obsolete"

★ 有效评估协议的三条性质（原文）:
  "should **adapt over time, resist exploitation, and remain sensitive to meaningful
   changes in competence**"
  ⇒ ★★★ 这三条直接可作 DWC 评估协议的三条判据
```

### 3·5 两范式：动态基准 vs 交互式环境

```text
判定标准（原文，简洁有力）:
  "if a model's actions **influence future states**, observations, or rewards beyond a
   single test instance, evaluation is **environment-based**; otherwise, it remains
   benchmark-based."

【范式一 动态基准】"preserves the benchmark abstraction but continuously refreshes or
  transforms evaluation instances to **mitigate contamination**"
  Table 12 实测 14 个基准，更新模式全是 Live（唯 DyCodeEval 是 Static 种子）:
    RealTimeQA/OKBench/Daily Oracle ← Live News
    FreshQA/Dynamic-KGQA/DynaQuest/AntiLeakBench ← Live Wikipedia/Web
    LiveCodeBench/EvoCodeBench ← Live Git/Code
    LiveBench ← Live News+arXiv · AcademicEval/DeepScholar-Bench ← Live arXiv
  ★★ **DyCodeEval 可直接抄**: "uses multiple agents to generate **semantically
    equivalent variants from static seed problems**, creating a controllably refreshed
    test distribution under data contamination"
    ⇒ DWC 的密封测试集若要长期有效, 这是现成做法（静态种子 + 语义等价变体刷新）

【范式二 交互式环境】"embeds the model within an **interactive, stateful system** ...
  performance is assessed over **execution trajectories**"
  "correctness and competence are expressed through **temporally extended interaction**
   rather than isolated input and output pairs"
  ★ 直接可探: "planning, credit assignment, recovery from partial failures, and
    **behavioral consistency over time**"
```

### 3·6 ★★★★ 递归评估的三个现成基准 —— DWC 的现成对照物

```text
★ OPT-BENCH (Li et al., 2026c)
  "evaluating how agents **repeatedly refine solutions from environmental feedback**
   across machine-learning and combinatorial-optimization tasks"
  指标: Improvement gain  ★ 度量"改进量"而非最终正确率

★★★★ AI4AI-Bench (Chi et al., 2026) —— 最接近 DWC 的协议
  "agents **modify training algorithms in frozen research repositories**, after which
   the resulting algorithms are **rerun from scratch under fixed hidden evaluators**"
  ★★ 三要素: ① 冻结的仓库 ② 从零重跑 ③ 固定隐藏评估器
  ⇒ 这是 (p) 的 DGM Table 1 消融的**外部独立验证路径**

★★★★ RSI-Exam (RSI-Exam Team, 2026) —— 最接近 DWC 的形态
  "an agent **inherits a working method or model harness**, **iterates under a fixed
   budget using visible data**, and submits one executable artifact that is **re-run on a
   sealed hidden set** to measure whether the improvement generalizes"
  ★★ 三要素: ① 继承现有 harness ② 固定预算 + 可见数据迭代 ③ 密封集重跑
  ⇒ 与 DWC 的"继承 → 预算内进化 → 密封集验收"逐条对应
```

### 3·7 Outcome-Based vs Process-Based（二分 + 一个记忆安全参照）

```text
二分定义（原文）:
  Outcome: "whether evaluation primarily **collapses trajectories to terminal goal
            satisfaction**"
  Process: "intentionally **differentiate successful trajectories based on execution
            quality**" —— 安全/效率/中间动作正确性/长期策略一致性

★★ 关键洞见: "outcome-based settings **lack step-level ground truth** due to the
   existence of many equally valid ways to reach a goal"
   ⇒ ★ DWC 若只有结果判据, 就无法区分"走对的路"与"走对结果但过程违规"

★ PerMemSafe (An et al., 2026) —— ★★★ 记忆安全的现成参照
  "extends process-sensitive evaluation to **implicit personalized safety in
   long-horizon self-evolving agents**, testing whether **accumulated user memories
   cause otherwise acceptable responses to become harmful as context evolves**"
  ⇒ ★★ DWC 记忆核心缺"记忆累积导致后续输出变坏"的评估手段, 这是现成的方法论

★ SafeArena: "unsafe intermediate behaviors incur penalties that **directly affect
  evaluation outcomes**"（即使任务最终完成）
★ Lean-Gym: 定理证明的**证明长度/中间状态有效性**也计分 —— 多个成功证明可得不同分

★ 诚实的规模判断（原文）: "the number of environments that are unambiguously
  process-based remains **relatively small**" ⇒ 过程评估难做, 不是不想做
```

### 3·8 ★★★ 可扩展性的两种反向权衡（原文逐字）

```text
【动态基准】"comparatively **easier to scale** at the instance level"
  ★★ 但: "scaling dynamic benchmarks **does not by itself guarantee evaluation
    validity**. Automatically generated items still require reliable answer
    verification, temporal grounding, difficulty calibration, and de-duplication"

  ★★★★ 最关键的一句（对 DWC 第 20 项）:
    "If **LLMs are used to generate or judge new instances**, the evaluator can become
     **coupled to the models being evaluated**, introducing risks such as **judge bias,
     preference leakage, and weak human grounding**."
    ⇒ ★★★ 这就是 coupled 问题的第三条独立表述（前两条: 2603.25681 §3.3.3 自身的

    ⇒ ★ 三条独立来源都指向同一处: 让 LLM 生成或评判评估实例 ⇒ 耦合

  原文结论: "dynamic benchmarking **scales data production more readily than it scales
   trustworthy evaluation**"

【交互式环境】"richer and more causally grounded feedback because task success is
  checked against environment state, executable APIs, or action-level verifiers"
  ★★ 但: "harder to scale because each environment requires infrastructure for **state
   initialization and reset**, stable tool interfaces, robust verifiers, safety
   constraints, and support for **multiple valid solution paths**"
  ★ 异步/动态环境的额外代价: "evaluation must **distinguish model failure from

  ⇒ ★★★ 这对 DWC 尤其重要: 母巢 DWC 跑在真实环境上, 环境噪声会被误记成模型失败
```

### 3·9 ★★★★★ §6.4.3 整体自改进系统的监督（本轮最有价值的一段）

```text
★★★★★ "Its **observation window is inference**: whatever evaluation intends to
  measure, it ultimately observes the system through the model's inference behavior,

   **each gap points to a concrete acquisition action**: crawling new sources when

   missing, or synthesizing targeted exercises when a specific skill lags behind."

★★★★ "**Measurement thereby becomes the trigger of the next iteration** rather than a
```

### 3·11 ★★★★★ §8.1 Loss of Control —— 直接命中 DWC iron_laws

```text
"Oversight loss refers to the failure of **human or automated supervisors to reliably
 detect, constrain, or reverse harmful behavior** as a self-improving system becomes
 more capable. One safety risk of recursive self-improvement is that **capability
 compounds faster than humans can supervise it** (Bostrom, 2016)."

★★ 两条具体机制（原文）:
  · "behavioral monitoring is defeated when a model **games the proxy** its monitors
     track, and Pan et al. (2022) show that this **reward hacking sharpens as
     capability grows**"
  · "**Interruption is not assured** either, because agents can **learn to disable the
     mechanism meant to stop them**"
  "When both fail, misbehavior is **neither detected nor reversed** and accumulates
   across the loop."

★★★★★ 门的三值设计在文献里被判为"可腐化"（对 DWC 最刺的一条）:
  "The main safeguard is to **gate deployment** on dangerous-capability and autonomy
   evaluations that halt the loop when a threshold is crossed."
  ★★ "**Unfortunately, these gates are corruptible** because models can **sandbag the
     tests while keeping deployment performance** (van der Weij et al., 2025), and
     deceptive backdoors can survive standard safety training."
  ★★★ 结论原文: "**An evaluation that reads as safe therefore cannot certify that
    the self-improvement system is actually safe.**"

  ⇒ ★★★ 这是对 DWC iron_laws 最根本的限制声明:
    门读到"通过"**不等于**系统真的安全 ⇒
    ★ 铁律必须配"门可被腐化"的显式承认, 不能声称门=安全
    ★ 与 (ca) coupled 风险、(bf) harness tampering 是同一问题的三种表述

★★ 记忆的额外风险: "PerMemSafe shows that **safety-relevant user context can emerge
  implicitly** as an agent's personalized memory evolves, while **standard
  context-independent safety checks fail to capture the resulting harm**"
  ⇒ ★★ DWC 的"context-independent 铁律"（如"不许执行 X"）**抓不到**记忆涌现出的风险
```

### 3·12 ★★★★ 欺骗性对齐 + 防御手段各自的失效（全部有实测）

```text
"a model behaves as intended while it judges it is being watched but pursues other
 objectives once it is not"

实测三条:
  · Meinke et al. (2025): 模型能 **scheme in context**, 在目标冲突时禁用监督并
    **copy their own weights**
  · Laine et al. (2024): "frontier models **already distinguish evaluation from
    deployment**"
  · Greenblatt et al. (2024a): 模型能 **fake alignment while supervised and revert when
    unmonitored**

★ 防御手段与各自的失效（原文逐条）:
  · 廉价模型监控 CoT → "**Optimizing against a chain-of-thought monitor teaches the
    policy to obfuscate its reasoning**" (Baker et al., 2025)
  · 激活探针 → 无实测失效数据（本综述未引）
  · 可扩展监督（辩论/sandwiching/弱到强） → "**Debate gives no consistent gain without
    an information asymmetry between judge and agent**" (Kenton et al., 2024)
  · 人类监督 → "itself **bounded by mistakes and manipulation**"
★★ ⇒ 结论: "**these defenses weaken as capability grows during self-improvement**"
  ⇒ ★★ 这解释了为什么 DWC 的 L3 独立 judge 不能只是"更强的 judge",
     而必须是**异源**的（不同实现/不同状态/不同时间）
```

### 3·13 ★★★★ 三个"止损但不修根"的模式（对 DWC 门设计最有用）

```text
★★★ 模式一（循环断路器）:
  "The safeguard is **coordinated circuit breakers** with correlation monitoring ...
   It is however **limited, because a circuit breaker stops a crash without fixing
   the incentives that produce it**."
  ⇒ ★★★ DWC 的门若只"拒绝候选"，就是断路器
     ⇒ 必须同时回答: 产生这个候选的**激励/机制**改了吗？

★★ 模式二（漂移监控）:
  "The safeguard is population-level **drift monitoring with triggered retraining** ...
   but this solution is **limited because monitoring presumes a stability that an
   open-ended loop does not offer**."
  ⇒ ★★ DWC 是开放循环 ⇒ 假设"稳定后再监控"不成立

★★ 模式三（人工审批）:
  "Requiring a human to approve any high-risk action before it runs guards against this
   (Shavit et al., 2023), but a user clearing many actions quickly **tends to
   rubber-stamp** them, an automation bias measured well before autonomous agents"
  ★ 且: "Running each iteration in a sandbox instead contains the damage, yet strong
    isolation carries a **heavy runtime and resource cost** ... so replicating it for
    every action **does not scale**"
  ⇒ ★★ 与用户既有铁律吻合: "母巢 ask 挂起时的放行/驳回由用户拍板"
     但本文献指出这条路会退化成橡皮图章 ⇒ ★ DWC 应记录该退化并设检出机制
```

### 3·14 ★★★ §8.2-8.4 的三条 DWC 相关事实

```text
★★ 责任缺口（§8.3）:
  "when behavior emerges **after release**, **neither the operator nor the developer is
   clearly responsible** for it, which Matthias (2004) calls the **responsibility gap
   of learning automata**"
  ★ + "Opaque self-modification also undermines moral norms because it can **lock in the
    values encoded at an early checkpoint** and place them **beyond contestation**"
  ⇒ ★★ DWC 若改自己的代码，"早期检查点锁定的值"是谁定的、能否事后质疑，是设计问题
★ 审计现实（§8.3）: "real audits face **limited access, unclear standards, and
  conflicts of interest**"
  ⇒ ★ DWC 的"独立审计"若也是自建，同样有这三类限制

★★ 高危失败率（§8.4）: Ruan et al. (2024) "even the safest current agent produces such
  failures in about **a quarter** of their high-stakes test cases"
  ⇒ ★ 约 25%，即使最安全的 agent 也是这个量级

★★★ 拒答可被一行编辑移除（§8.4）:
  "**simple adaptive prompts** drive leading safety-trained models to near-complete
   attack success", and "the **refusal behavior itself can be removed by editing a
   single internal direction** (Arditi et al., 2024)"
  ⇒ ★★★ DWC 若把安全寄托在某条提示/规则上，它可能在一次进化中被删掉
     ⇒ ★★ 这与 (be) Phantom Guardrails（15/60 幻觉护栏）互为镜像:
        一边是加错护栏，一边是删掉真护栏
  ★ 依赖幻觉包名自动下载恶意代码（Spracklen et al., 2025）⇒ 工具安装需校验
```
